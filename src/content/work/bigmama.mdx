## Context

A reporting app for people exposing illegal land deals in Zimbabwe. They are often watched, and
their devices can be seized.

## Decisions

I started with a threat model, including **what the app cannot protect against**.

<Note kind="decision">
  The UI may not say "military-grade encryption". The allowed wording is written down, because
  someone who overestimates their protection takes risks they shouldn't.
</Note>

## Build

- Reports are encrypted in the browser (AES-GCM-256) before sending.
- Submissions queue offline and are sent exactly once, however flaky the network.
- One control wipes everything the app stored on the device.
- A 300 KB size budget enforced in CI, because on metered data, size decides who can use it.

## Where it stands

Live, with security scanning in CI. Next: stripping photo metadata on the device, since it can
identify a reporter.
