## Context

A merchant in Harare might get paid through EcoCash, ZIPIT, a bank transfer, Paynow and cash, all
on the same day. Some of that money arrives, some is pending, and some just disappears.

## Decisions

The whole product answers one question:

> "How much money did I actually receive today, and where is it stuck?"

<Note kind="decision">
  **Kill criterion:** if merchants won't log transactions by hand, the product is invalid and gets
  killed. That outcome counts as success. The MVP exists to test behaviour, not to scale.
</Note>

So there's no bank integration, no automation, and nothing clever. That's the point. If people won't
do the simple version by hand, automating it answers the wrong question.

## Build

A small FastAPI backend with a pure ledger core. Each transaction records amount, channel,
direction and status (expected, received, pending, missing), with an optional screenshot. A daily
"truth" view groups the day by status and channel, and flags anything expected for more than 24
hours. I deprecated the frontend so the test stayed focused on the backend and the behaviour.

## Where it stands

Success criteria are written: logging takes under 30 seconds, the daily screen needs no
explanation, and the app survives seven days of real use. It's here because the discipline matters
more than the code: decide in advance what would prove you wrong.
