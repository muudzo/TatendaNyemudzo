## Context

A merchant in Harare can be paid through EcoCash, ZIPIT, a bank, Paynow and cash on the same
day. Some of that money arrives, some is pending, some disappears.

## Decisions

The whole product answers one question:

> "How much money did I actually receive today, and where is it stuck?"

<Note kind="decision">
  **Kill criterion:** if merchants won't log transactions by hand, the product dies. That result
  counts as success.
</Note>

## Build

A small FastAPI ledger: log a payment, then see the day grouped by status and channel, with
anything overdue by 24 hours flagged. No integrations, on purpose.

## Where it stands

Success means logging takes under 30 seconds and the app survives seven days of real use. The
discipline is the point: decide in advance what would prove you wrong.
